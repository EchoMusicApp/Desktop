# Privacy Policy — Echo Music Desktop

Echo Music Desktop is designed to keep your listening data on your own device.

## Local Data

All of the following are stored locally on your machine and are never uploaded to Echo Music servers:

- **Account cookies & session state** (used only to authenticate directly with YouTube Music)
- **Settings, equalizer presets, and theme preferences**
- **Cached tracks, offline downloads, and local listening statistics**
- **Local log files** (contain no passwords or cookies)

## Optional Anonymous Install Ping

On first launch, Echo Music Desktop asks whether you want to send **one** anonymous install ping. Nothing is sent unless you explicitly opt in.

- **Just ping**: Sends a single random identifier and no system information.
- **Send details**: Includes your OS (`darwin`, `windows`, `linux`), CPU architecture (`arm64`, `amd64`), and Echo Music Desktop version.
- You can review or change this setting at any time under **Settings → Privacy**.

## Crash Reports

Crash reporting is **off** by default. If you enable **Crash reports** in **Settings → Privacy**, crash stack traces are scrubbed of passwords, cookies, and web URLs before being sent.
